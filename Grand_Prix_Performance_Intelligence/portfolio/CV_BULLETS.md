# CV Entry

**Grand Prix Performance Intelligence | Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Python**

- Built a Formula 1 performance report from 12 relational CSV tables (98,740 source rows), covering driver and constructor performance, season trends, circuits, qualifying and race outcomes.
- Cleaned and transformed the source tables in Power Query, created a calendar table, and modelled relationships between race results, drivers, constructors, circuits and season standings.
- Created DAX measures for wins, points, podiums, win rate, non-classified results, position change and year-over-year comparisons.
- Checked key findings against the source CSVs with Python, including Red Bull's 21 wins from 22 race events in 2023 and the difference between blank finishing positions and explicit DNF labels.
- Documented the model, KPI definitions, source limitations and data-quality decisions for reproducibility.
