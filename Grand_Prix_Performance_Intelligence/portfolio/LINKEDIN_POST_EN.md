# LinkedIn Post — English

I recently completed a Formula 1 performance analysis project using Power BI, Power Query and DAX.

I worked with 12 CSV tables from F1DB and built the report around a few practical questions: how drivers and constructors perform across seasons, how well qualifying position turns into finishing position, and what the results can tell us about reliability.

One thing I paid attention to was the meaning of the data, not just the charts. For example, 39.67% of result rows have a blank numeric finishing position, while 31.88% are explicitly labelled `DNF`. Those are different groups, so I kept the measures separate.

A result that stood out was Red Bull winning 21 of 22 races in 2023 (95.45% win share) in the dataset snapshot.

The repository includes the CSV data, Power Query files, DAX measures, model documentation, data checks and selected screenshots from my Power BI work.
